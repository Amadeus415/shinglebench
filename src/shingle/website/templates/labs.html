Find out if your model can run a business before your customers do.

We evaluate frontier and open-weight models on long, realistic business-building tasks, graded by blind humans and calibrated LLM judges. You get scores, plus every artifact and trajectory behind them.

Pre-release evaluation

Private runs on the held-out suite before launch, under NDA. Results stay with you until you choose to publish, and nothing is shared with other labs.

You get scores with confidence intervals, a per-dimension breakdown, failure analysis, and run bundles for every episode.

Custom brief suites

Briefs written for your market: your geographies, languages, price points and compliance rules. Useful for deciding which model to deploy in an agent product that touches commerce.

You get a versioned private task suite, a rubric adapted to your domain, and repeatable rounds.

Grading data

Blind human judgments (absolute and pairwise) with rationales, paired with frozen businesses and full trajectories. Useful for training and validating graders and reward models.

You get licensed datasets, agreement statistics and a judge–human calibration report.

How an engagement works

  1. We agree on models, suites and budgets. Every model gets identical scaffolding, which we document.
  2. Episodes run in our sandbox, which has no network access, with simulated ads, payments and suppliers.
  3. Automated checks run, then LLM judges, then blind human graders, with gold items and attention checks.
  4. You receive a report like Report 01, plus the raw results and bundles.

We do not take payment to rank models on the public leaderboard. Commercial engagements never change public results.
